MAJOR DRIVERS OF CHURN - RANKED BY IMPORTANCE
================================================================================

1. TOTAL VIEWING HOURS (Importance: 23.9%)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   • STRONGEST predictor of churn
   • Churned customers: 66.6 hours/month (average)
   • Retained customers: 83.2 hours/month (average)
   • DIFFERENCE: -16.6 hours/month (-20% less viewing)
   • Statistical Significance: p < 0.001 (HIGHLY significant)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Low engagement is the #1 red flag. Customers who watch 
   less than 50 hours/month have a 71% churn rate vs. 13% for high viewers.

2. AVERAGE SESSION DURATION (Importance: 21.1%)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   • SECOND strongest predictor
   • Churned customers: 49.4 minutes/session (average)
   • Retained customers: 57.8 minutes/session (average)
   • DIFFERENCE: -8.3 minutes (-14% shorter sessions)
   • Statistical Significance: p < 0.001 (HIGHLY significant)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Short sessions indicate lack of content engagement. Users
   who don't complete episodes/movies are more likely to cancel.

3. NUMBER OF UNIQUE TITLES WATCHED (Importance: 14.5%)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   • Indicates content variety and exploration
   • Churned customers: 19.5 titles/month (average)
   • Retained customers: 23.7 titles/month (average)
   • DIFFERENCE: -4.3 titles (-18% less variety)
   • Statistical Significance: p < 0.001 (HIGHLY significant)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Customers who explore diverse content stay longer. Limited
   content exploration suggests they're not finding what they want.

4. CUSTOMER SERVICE INTERACTIONS (Importance: 12.5%)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   • CRITICAL negative indicator
   • Churned customers: 3.18 interactions/year (average)
   • Retained customers: 2.49 interactions/year (average)
   • DIFFERENCE: +0.69 interactions (+28% more issues)
   • Statistical Significance: p < 0.001 (HIGHLY significant)
   
   CHURN RATE BY CS INTERACTIONS:
   • 1 interaction:  30% churn rate
   • 2 interactions: 35% churn rate
   • 3 interactions: 40% churn rate
   • 4 interactions: 47% churn rate
   • 5+ interactions: 58% churn rate
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Customer service interactions are a strong churn predictor.
   Each additional contact increases churn likelihood. This suggests either
   unresolved issues OR problems with the service itself.

5. BINGE WATCHING SESSIONS (Importance: 10.5%)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   • Measures deep engagement
   • Churned customers: 6.2 sessions/month (average)
   • Retained customers: 7.7 sessions/month (average)
   • DIFFERENCE: -1.5 sessions (-19% fewer binges)
   • Statistical Significance: p < 0.001 (HIGHLY significant)
   
   KEY INSIGHT: Binge-watching is healthy! It indicates users are highly
   engaged with content. Loss of binge behavior precedes churn.

CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The analysis reveals that CHURN IS PRIMARILY DRIVEN BY LOW ENGAGEMENT, not
price sensitivity. The strongest predictors are:

1. Low viewing hours
2. Short session durations
3. Limited content exploration
4. Unresolved customer service issues

The data strongly suggests that customers leave because they're not finding
value in the content, not because it's too expensive. In fact, Premium users
(highest price) have the LOWEST churn.

Focus should be on:
✓ Increasing user engagement through better content recommendations
✓ Resolving customer issues quickly and effectively
✓ Providing compelling content across all genres
✓ Identifying and intervening with at-risk users early

The good news: These are all addressable through product improvements and
operational excellence, rather than requiring price cuts that would hurt revenue.
